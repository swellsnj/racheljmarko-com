Why Mandalas? Why Not?

A Lifelong Love of Patterns

For 45 years, I’ve been drawn to patterns and design. Mandalas are my way of capturing that essence in a satisfying circle.

Sharing the Joy

I’ve been creating mandalas for most of my life, and since 2017, I’ve been teaching others to discover their own artistic expression through this calming practice.

My Neurodivergent Brain

My journey has been filled with twists and turns. Working for the man wasn’t fulfilling, but I made the best of it, balancing my love for art with the need to make a living.

A New Chapter

A layoff in March 2024 was a turning point. The stress lifted, and I realized it was time to fully embrace my artistic passion.

A Journey of Gratitude

I’m so grateful for this new adventure. I look forward to sharing my love of mandalas with my community and connecting with new people.

Thank You

To my husband, family, and chosen family, your unwavering support has been my constant.

I love you!
